# Profil çerçevesi görseli

Dosya: `avatar-frames-v1.png` — 1448 × 1086, şeffaf PNG, dört sütun ve üç satır.
Araç: yerleşik `image_gen`, düzenleme modu, `transparent_background: true`.
Referans: kullanıcının onayladığı 12 çerçeveli tasarım görseli.
Profil fotoğrafları bu görsele dahil değildir; uygulama kullanıcının fotoğrafını ayrı gösterir.

## Kullanılan üretim istemi

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create a production-ready TRANSPARENT PNG SPRITE ATLAS of ONLY the twelve decorative avatar frames from the reference image. Reference role: design/style reference for the 12 frames; remove ALL faces, people, portraits, text, cards, headings, navy backgrounds and labels. Preserve the distinct high-quality appearance of the 12 frames faithfully, including material detail, silver/gold/ice/chrome, violet orbit, cobalt manga accents, burgundy flower and coral/ivory koi. Layout is CRITICAL: exact 4 columns by 3 rows of EQUALLY SIZED SQUARE cells, full canvas aspect ratio 4:3, no margins/gutters between cells. All cell centers are at exactly ((col+0.5)/4,(row+0.5)/3) of the image. Each cell contains one centered frame. The frame's open center is a TRUE TRANSPARENT CIRCULAR HOLE with diameter exactly 62.5% of the square cell width, empty space with alpha=0, showing no face, no disk, no fill, no checkerboard, no colored backdrop. Inner aperture is consistent across all twelve, so any real user photo placed behind it will remain fully visible. All ornaments must stay OUTSIDE that open circular aperture. Overall ornament bounds fit within 92% of each cell, leaving a tiny transparent safety area. Nothing touches adjacent cells. Row1 col1 Platin thin broken brushed silver rim; col2 Yorunge violet asymmetrical orbit arcs+small satellite; col3 Editor broad matte ivory rim+tiny black bottom tab; col4 Kirik Halo cracked graphite segmented ring with ice-blue light in gaps. Row2 col1 Murekkep irregular white ink brush ring; col2 Panel four black-and-white manga corners with fine blue accents outside circular opening, no recognizable portrait drawings inside corner graphics; col3 Kristal faceted icy aqua glass polygonal ring; col4 Krom Akis flowing asymmetric mirror chrome ring with subtle iridescence. Row3 col1 Defne two curved golden laurel branches, open at top/bottom; col2 Krizantem sculpted burgundy and gold flower petal ring; col3 Koi two decorative ivory/coral fish and teal water around circle; col4 Yildiz Muhru sapphire silver star-compass ring. Make the 12 holes exactly aligned and same diameter. Every background pixel and every inner hole is genuinely transparent. NO avatar, no skin, no hair, no eyes, no hands, no people, no typography, no grid lines, no opaque white or black or navy fill anywhere except the actual material of a frame. High-resolution crisp cutout asset, straight-on view. Output only the transparent frame atlas.

## Uygulama notu

Görsel eşit boyutlu hücreler varsayılarak kırpılmaz. `lib/avatar-frame-geometry.mjs` her çerçevenin gerçek sınırlarını ve fotoğraf açıklığını ayrı tutar. Fotoğraf dairesi, kapalı açıklığın tüm şeffaf piksellerini kapsar ve kenarın altında küçük bir örtüşme bırakır. `avatar-frame.tsx` bu ölçülerle SVG görünüm alanını hizalar; komşu süslemeler ayrı SVG alanıyla kırpılır. Kaynak PNG değiştirilmemiştir.
